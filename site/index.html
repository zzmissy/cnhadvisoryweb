Build for what comes next.

As a business evolves, its original structure may no longer support its future. We help business owners prepare for growth, ownership changes, a future sale, or a transition to the next generation.

Planning may include estate freezes, family trusts, corporate reorganizations, amalgamations, windups, QSBC share purification, succession planning, intergenerational business transfers, and corporate tax risk reviews.

Plan beyond the practice.

A professional corporation can support more than current income. With thoughtful planning, it can become part of a broader strategy for investments, retirement, family wealth, and legacy.

Planning may include professional corporation structuring, corporate reorganizations, family trusts, prescribed rate loans, wealth extraction, insurance planning, succession planning, and tax health reviews.

Plan before opportunity becomes exposure.

Stock options, equity compensation, executive benefits, and career transitions can create significant tax decisions. The right planning helps clarify timing, liquidity requirements, available deductions, and the wider effect on personal wealth.

Planning may include stock option exercise planning, equity compensation reviews, cross-border tax planning, liquidity event planning, prescribed rate loans, estate planning, and general tax consulting.

Protect what matters across generations.

Family wealth planning involves more than reducing tax. It requires thoughtful coordination between financial priorities, family relationships, estate objectives, and the professionals responsible for carrying the plan forward.

Planning may include family trusts, prescribed rate loans, post-mortem tax planning, estate freezes, legacy planning, insurance strategies, cross-border planning, and the transfer of family business interests.
