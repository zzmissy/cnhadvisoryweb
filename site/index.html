Our Services

Wealth, tax and estate planning, from strategy to implementation.

Wealth Planning

Make significant financial decisions with a clear view of your wealth. We connect your personal and corporate finances to plan for major purchases, liquidity needs and the commitments that matter to you.

Tax Structuring

Preserve more of what you have built through thoughtful tax planning. We design and implement corporate reorganizations, ownership structures and capital gains strategies around your personal and business goals.

Estate Planning

Provide for the people who matter to you, on your terms. We bring together trusts, ownership and tax planning to guide how your wealth is preserved and passed on.

Succession & Exit

Prepare your business and personal wealth for what comes next. Whether you are selling or passing ownership to family, we structure the transition around your financial goals.

Insurance Solutions

Protect what you have built and prepare for the unexpected. We integrate personal and corporate insurance into your wealth plan to support succession, tax planning and risk management.

Philanthropic Planning

Make giving a meaningful part of your wealth plan. We structure charitable gifts, donor advised funds and private foundations to support the causes you value during your lifetime and beyond.

How We Work

Your relationship. Our expertise.
Extend the planning support you offer your clients. We lead the planning and implementation, with your involvement tailored to the relationship.

  1. A simple introduction starts the process. We arrange the initial conversation and establish how you would like to be involved.

  2. We lead the discovery, gather information and identify planning opportunities. We confirm the scope, fees and deliverables before proceeding.

  3. We develop the strategy and lead your client through the recommendations. You can participate in key discussions as we help your client make informed decisions.

  4. We manage implementation and, with your client’s permission, keep you informed. We carry the agreed work through to completion.

  5. Once the engagement is complete, we return the ongoing client relationship to you, with a clear summary and supporting documentation. We remain available whenever further planning support is needed.

Personal guidance. Practical action.
Work directly with us to bring your wealth, tax and estate priorities into one coordinated plan.

  1. Tell us about your circumstances, priorities and the decisions ahead. We establish where our expertise can make a difference.

  2. We assess your needs and propose a tailored engagement. You receive a clear scope, fee and outline of deliverables.

  3. We present your strategy and explain the choices and implications. Together, we establish the actions that fit your goals.

  4. We carry your approved strategy through to implementation. We confirm what has been completed and identify any future review needs.

  5. We revisit your plan as your circumstances and priorities evolve. Together, we assess progress, reflect on what has changed and refine the strategy where needed.
